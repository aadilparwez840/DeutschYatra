The no-surprises version

Dream bigger.
Plan truer.

Germany can be a brilliant choice—but “free education” is only one part of the picture. We help you see the whole one.

Tuition isn't the full cost

Public universities often charge no tuition, but semester fees, living costs, insurance and your first-year funds still matter.

Language opens doors

Many Master's programmes are in English. German makes Bachelor's options, daily life and student jobs stronger.

APS is a real milestone

Most Indian applicants need APS verification. Treat it as an early, trackable step—not an afterthought.

Your degree is a beginning

Graduates may apply for up to 18 months to seek qualified work after completing a German degree.

Current rules can change. Verify requirements with official sources.
